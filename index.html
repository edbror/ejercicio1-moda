Drift

A generative ambient instrument. Every sound is synthesized live — no samples, no recordings. Underneath runs a slowly evolving weather system: it breathes and modulates on its own.

The sliders below set your wishes. The glowing marker on each shows where the system actually drifted — you can bias the weather, but you can't command it. Press Gust (or G) to shove it somewhere new.

Or hand the controls to the sky: Cosmos syncs the instrument to live space weather — real solar-wind speed, magnetic field and geomagnetic storm data measured a million miles out at the L1 point. The Sun chooses the key; a geomagnetic substorm re-keys the music.

## *Drift*
### A generative sound work in which the Sun chooses the key

**An infinite, unrepeatable soundscape — synthesised live, never recorded —
that you can influence but never command. In its synced state, the work tunes
itself to real-time solar-wind data measured a million miles from Earth.**

---

**Opening 2026 · Duration: infinite · Admission free · Headphones provided**

A new sound work, *Drift*, proposes a quiet but radical question: what remains
of authorship once the author admits they are not in charge?

*Drift* is a self-evolving sonic environment that runs entirely in the browser.
It contains no recordings of any kind. Every sound — struck bells, swelling
pads, the reverberant space itself — is synthesised in the instant of listening
and never recurs in the same form. There is no score, no loop, and no ending.
The piece does not document a process; it *is* one, continuous and indifferent
to its audience.

Visitors are given seven controls and invited to shape the "weather" of the
sound. But the instrument does not obey. Each gesture is treated as a *wish*,
not a command: the system blends the visitor's input with its own autonomous
drift and continually returns toward an equilibrium no one has set. A glowing
marker on every control reveals where each value has *actually* gone — making
the gap between intention and outcome visible, moment to moment. "You are not
the composer," the work proposes. "You are weather pressure. You lean on the
system, and it leans back."

The piece then performs its most striking gesture: it surrenders the tonality
of the music to the Sun. In its synced state, *Drift* abandons its internal
weather and tunes itself to **live space-weather telemetry** — solar wind,
interplanetary magnetic field, and geomagnetic storm index — measured by
spacecraft at the **L1 Lagrange point**, roughly 1.5 million kilometres
sunward of Earth. When the Sun's magnetic field turns southward — the same
physical event that ignites the aurora — the harmony darkens. A geomagnetic
substorm does not merely influence the music; it **re-keys** it, forcing the
work into a new tonal centre without consent. The data arrives about an hour
old: visitors hear not the present, but a message already in transit.

*Drift* stages the contemporary condition of acting within systems too large,
too slow, and too entangled to command — climate, network, market, sky — where
our inputs are real but never decisive. Against an aesthetics of mastery, it
proposes one of **tending**: the artist not as origin but as one pressure among
many in a field that exceeds them.

**Technical note.** The work is built on the Web Audio API with no external
dependencies and no backend; the visitor's own device performs both the
synthesis and the live data retrieval. Space-weather data is provided by the
NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (DSCOVR / ACE). When the feeds are
unavailable, the instrument continues to generate its own weather. The work is
most fully itself when left alone.

### Quick facts
- **Title:** *Drift*
- **Year:** 2026
- **Medium:** Generative sound; live Web Audio synthesis; real-time
  heliophysical telemetry
- **Duration:** Infinite (non-repeating)
- **Recordings used:** None
- **Data source:** NOAA SWPC — solar wind & geomagnetic indices, retrieved at L1
- **Controls:** Seven macro parameters (density, brightness, register, spread,
  motion, space, warmth); *Gust*; *Cosmos* (sync to the sky)
